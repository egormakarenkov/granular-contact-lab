# Scope and provenance

This local release draft contains a curated implementation of generic frictionless contact mechanics and an interactive numerical workbench. The numerical solver and interface were developed with AI coding assistance. The runtime does not call an AI service.

The draft was assembled using a source-file allowlist. Imported conversations, supplied reference HTML/code, personal planning documents, screenshots of private work, prior run archives, workstation logs, prior verification reports with absolute paths, and earlier numerical claims were not copied.

The supplied examples were computed afresh by the included generator. Seeds 2027, 2028 and 2029 are synthetic fixture choices. Their generation recipes, exact initial conditions and outcomes are supplied. There are no physical measurements or third-party datasets.

Reference-specific frozen shrink interactions, changing-radius controls and orbital control recipes are omitted. The public intruder stays rigid. Only translation, hold and a windowed lateral sine motion are exposed. Text and UI do not reproduce private research questions, conjectures, interpretations, attribution or endorsements.

This boundary is a content-curation record, not a determination of intellectual-property ownership. Any future addition of unpublished research ideas, supplied third-party code, correspondence or personal attribution needs the appropriate permission and provenance review before inclusion.
