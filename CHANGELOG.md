# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — initial curated prototype

Prepared 2026-11-24.

- Dimensionless 2D normal-contact velocity QP with damping, optional scaled inertia, and geometric/residual checks.
- Rigid prescribed intruder with hold, translation and lateral sway.
- Contact inspection, force bounds, matrix rank/SVD, playback and reaction/work plots.
- Identical-state comparisons, paused-frame forks, parameter maps and bounded search with timestep/seed checks.
- Eight fresh synthetic recordings, analytic support and redundant-constraint examples, and a standalone replay.
- Figures, CSV, replay HTML and numerical reproduction bundles.
- Portable Windows EXE and launcher/build source.
- Diagnostic panels clear when the selected recorded state changes.

Status: experimental numerical prototype; no physical validation or global optimality claim.
